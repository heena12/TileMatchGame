Tile Pop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how the Tile Pop: Match & Explore mobile game (package com.tilematchgame, "the app"), published by Sofscript ("we", "us"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or transmit personal data to our servers. The app's internet permission is used only for optional cloud save, if you turn it on.

Tile Pop does not ask you to sign up and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or location. The app does not contain advertising and does not use third-party analytics.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, boosters and settings (such as sound and language) are stored only on your device. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules daily reminders locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.

Cloud save (optional)

If cloud save is switched on, only your game progress is backed up so you can restore it. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Data sharing

We never sell your data and we do not share it with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
